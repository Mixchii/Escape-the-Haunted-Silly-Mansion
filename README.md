# Escape-the-Haunted-Silly-Mansion
Trapped in the Grumbleton Mansion, you must help three embarrassed ghosts to escape! Soothe a sneezing ghost, cure a hiccuping one, and find light for a knight afraid of the dark. Solve silly puzzles, collect Good Haunting Badges, and talk to Mr. Wobbles the butler to unlock the door. Type your commands and outsmart the spooky fun!
